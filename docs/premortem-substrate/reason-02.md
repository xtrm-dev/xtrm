Stage 0 ate the budget. Stage 0 is the foundation: "shared daemon, single SQLite over Unix socket, project registration, file-lock launch, schema registry, transaction serialization, multi-client coordination." Every other stage (1 through 13) is serially dependent on it. The doc cites the bd→Dolt networked-store lesson as motivation, but the proposed daemon is still a from-scratch custom IPC layer: Unix socket protocol, schema migrations, transactional writes across five domain owners (core/substrate/channels/specialists/console), idempotency for emitter/pulse, identity stability across daemon restarts. Six months in, either (a) the daemon ate most of the calendar — the team has a robust daemon but stages 1–4 each got 2 weeks instead of 6 and are unfinished and brittle, or (b) the daemon was declared "good enough" at month 2, and stages 1–4 are now patching around its rough edges (race conditions on container open, schema migrations that require daemon restart and orphan jobs, socket-protocol versioning hell).
